# [Domain-7](#domain-7-hr-information-management) **HR Information Management**

- **10% weighting.** Tools, technology, and systems that efficiently optimize the organization's access to its human resource data

## HR Information Systems (HRIS)

- Understanding how an organization's HRIS supports core HR processes — recordkeeping, reporting, and data accuracy — and the operational role HR plays in maintaining clean, compliant employee data
- Recognizing data privacy and confidentiality obligations tied to employee records (e.g., medical information requiring separate, restricted storage from general personnel files)

## Applying HR technology and data

- Using HR systems and data appropriately to support the metrics and reporting needs referenced across other domains (Business Management's HR metrics, Total Rewards administration, Employee Engagement tracking)
- Recognizing basic risks of HR technology use — data security, access control, and record-retention requirements — at an operational, correctly-implementing-existing-systems level rather than designing organization-wide technology strategy
