# [Domain-4](#domain-4-total-rewards) **Total Rewards**

- **15% weighting.** Implementing, promoting, and managing compensation and benefit programs that attract and retain talent

## Compensation administration

- Administering pay structures, incentive programs, and pay practices consistent with existing organizational policy and applicable law (FLSA classification and overtime rules are especially frequently tested here, connecting directly back to Domain 1's compliance content)
- Recognizing situations where inconsistent compensation administration creates both morale and legal risk (e.g., pay equity concerns)

## Benefits administration

- Implementing and communicating benefits programs — health, retirement, and other offerings — and understanding baseline compliance obligations connected to benefits administration (e.g., COBRA continuation rights, ERISA-governed plan basics)

## Promoting total rewards to attract and retain talent

- Understanding how the total rewards package (compensation + benefits + non-monetary rewards) functions as a recruitment and retention tool — correctly communicating the value of the full package, not just base pay, to employees and candidates
