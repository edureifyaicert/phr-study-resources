# [Domain-1](#domain-1-business-management) **Business Management**

- **14% weighting.** Using information about the organization and business environment to reinforce expectations, influence decision-making, and avoid risk — the operational context every other domain sits inside

## Interpreting and using business and HR data

- Reading and applying organizational data (financial statements, workforce metrics) to support HR decisions and recommendations — an operational-level analytical skill, not strategic forecasting (that's SPHR's territory)
- **HR metrics**: attrition rates, diversity in hiring, time-to-hire, time-to-fill, ROI, and training success measures — knowing what each metric actually measures and when it's the relevant one to cite

## Legal and compliance fundamentals

- **Federal employment law frameworks**: FLSA, FMLA, ADA, Title VII, and NLRA form the compliance backbone referenced throughout this domain and reappear across nearly every other functional area — PHR expects correctly recognizing which law applies to a described workplace situation
- Risk management from a compliance perspective: recognizing situations that create legal exposure and knowing the correct operational response to reduce that risk

## HR's role in organizational development

- Understanding how HR practices support and reinforce the organization's broader operational goals — at the PHR level, this means correctly implementing initiatives that were designed elsewhere, not designing organizational strategy
