# [Domain-5](#domain-5-employee-engagement) **Employee Engagement**

- **17% weighting — the second-heaviest domain**, and the newest standalone functional area introduced in the 2024 ECO refresh; developing, communicating, and enhancing employee engagement initiatives to support optimal performance throughout the employee lifecycle

## Engagement initiatives across the employee lifecycle

- Implementing engagement programs at each stage of the employee lifecycle — onboarding, ongoing engagement, and offboarding — rather than treating engagement as a single isolated HR program
- Recognizing common drivers of disengagement described in a scenario and selecting the correct operational response (e.g., recognition, communication, workload adjustment) before it escalates into a retention problem

## Performance management activities

- Supporting fair, consistent performance management processes as a component of engagement — employees who experience inconsistent or unclear performance feedback disengage faster, a link PHR scenarios frequently test
- Correctly documenting performance issues and engagement-related concerns in a way that's both legally defensible and genuinely supportive of the employee's development
