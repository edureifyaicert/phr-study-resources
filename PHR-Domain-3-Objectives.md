## Implementing and evaluating L&D programs

- Contributing to the organization's learning and development activities by **implementing and evaluating programs**, rather than designing organization-wide learning strategy (an SPHR-level responsibility)
- Correctly matching a training delivery method to the actual need described in a scenario — a common PHR trap is selecting a generic training solution without first confirming it addresses the specific gap presented

## Internal consultation and data

- **Providing internal consultation**: supporting managers and employees in identifying development needs
- **Providing data**: reporting on training completion, effectiveness, and ROI to support decision-making — connects back to Domain 1's HR metrics content
