- **14% weighting.** Identifying, attracting, and employing talent while following all federal laws related to the hiring process

## Legal compliance in hiring

- Applying U.S. federal laws and organizational policy to hiring: **Title VII** (non-discrimination), nepotism policy, **disparate impact** (a facially neutral practice that disproportionately affects a protected group), **FLSA** classification issues, and independent contractor classification — correctly distinguishing lawful from unlawful hiring practices in a described scenario is a core tested skill

## Sourcing and the talent acquisition lifecycle

- **Sourcing methods and techniques**: identifying appropriate channels to attract qualified talent for a given role
- **Managing the talent acquisition lifecycle**: interviews, job offers, background checks, job descriptions, onboarding, orientation, and skills assessments — end-to-end operational execution of the hiring process
- **Interviewing and selection techniques**: structured interview methods, and understanding reliability/validity concepts as they apply to selection tools

## Workforce planning fundamentals

- **Planning concepts**: succession planning and forecasting at an operational level — supporting a workforce plan rather than designing overall organizational talent strategy
- **Staffing alternatives**: outsourcing and temporary employment as options when standard hiring isn't the right fit
- **Metrics to assess staffing effectiveness**: cost per hire, selection ratios, and adverse impact analysis (commonly tested via the "4/5ths rule" for identifying potential adverse impact in selection outcomes)
