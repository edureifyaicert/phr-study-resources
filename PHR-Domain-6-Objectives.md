# [Domain-6](#domain-6-employee-and-labor-relations) **Employee & Labor Relations**

- **20% weighting — the single heaviest domain on the exam.** Managing, monitoring, and/or promoting legally compliant programs and policies that affect the employee experience; deserves the largest share of your study time

## Employee relations practice

- **Grievance handling and workplace investigations**: conducting fair, consistent, well-documented investigations — a heavily scenario-tested skill, since inconsistent investigation practice is a common source of legal exposure
- **Progressive discipline**: applying disciplinary policy consistently across similarly situated employees — PHR scenarios frequently test whether a described disciplinary action was applied fairly and defensibly

## Labor relations basics

- Understanding union environments and collective bargaining fundamentals, relevant even for HR professionals working in non-union workplaces, since labor relations awareness (including union-organizing activity indicators) is explicitly part of the tested content
- Recognizing employee rights under the **NLRA**, including protections that can apply even in non-union settings (e.g., protected concerted activity)

## Legal compliance in the ongoing employment relationship

- Applying **Title VII, ADA, ADEA, FLSA, and FMLA** correctly to described post-hire employment situations — leave requests, accommodation requests, and termination decisions are common scenario setups
- Recognizing the difference between **disparate treatment** (intentional discrimination) and **disparate impact** (a neutral practice with a discriminatory effect) — a foundational distinction this domain returns to repeatedly
